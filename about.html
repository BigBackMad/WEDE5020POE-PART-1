About Little Woods Books

Founded in the heart of Cape Town, Little Woods Books was born from a simple belief: that everyone should have access to the magic of a good story without breaking the bank.

We specialize in curated "staff picks" and high-quality secondhand books, ensuring every title on our shelf is worth your time. From trending fiction to hidden gems, our goal is to help you find your next favorite read for between R95 and R149.
